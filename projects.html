Restaurant Section Notification System

Two ESP32 boards. A transmitter has six buttons, one for each section of a restaurant floor. A receiver has six timer lights. Pressing a section button starts the matching light. I plan to start with WiFi and MQTT messaging, and fall back to LoRa or nRF24L01+ radios if the range is too long for WiFi.
